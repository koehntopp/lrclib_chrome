**Quick Lyrics** – find song lyrics fast, across multiple sources.

Looking for a song's lyrics? Click the extension icon to open a search box that already suggests your last search term. Use the clipboard button to paste in copied text when you need it – nothing is read automatically, you stay in control.

**Searches on demand:**
- lrclib.net
- AZLyrics
- Genius
- Google (`lyrics <search term>`)

That way you'll still find a match even if one particular database doesn't have the song.

No data is sent to third parties, no trackers – everything stays local in your browser.
